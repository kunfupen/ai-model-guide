## When to reach for Gemini 4 Neon

Gemini 4 Neon (November 18, 2026) is the middle model of Google's Gemini 4
generation. It sits between the low-cost
[Gemini 4 Hydrogen](/models/google-gemini-4-hydrogen) and the top-end
[Gemini 4 Carbon](/models/google-gemini-4-carbon). Google pitches it as a
cheaper frontier model for coding and enterprise workloads. It is the
generation's fourth model in about a month.

- Coding and agentic work that needs more than Hydrogen, at a lower token price
  than Carbon or [Gemini 4 Argon](/models/google-gemini-4-argon) ($4 / $20).
- Long-context jobs across a 1M-token window.
- Gemini app and Antigravity users on paid plans, where it is rolling out
  alongside the API.

Google has not said it replaces Argon or Carbon, so both stay current here.

## Specs & cost

- **Model ID:** `gemini-4-neon`, as reported by API listings and launch
  coverage.
- **Context:** 1,048,576 tokens. Listings give up to 65,536 output tokens.
- **Pricing:** $3 / 1M input, $15 / 1M output (thinking tokens bill as output),
  $0.15 / 1M implicitly cached input. No free API tier. Batch and long-context
  rates are not confirmed yet.
- **Availability:** the Gemini API in Google AI Studio, Gemini for business, and
  the Gemini app and Antigravity for paid subscribers.

> window and price above are where several independent listings agree.
