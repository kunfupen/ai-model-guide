## When to reach for GPT-6.2 Astra

GPT-6.2 Astra (November 17, 2026) is OpenAI's new flagship. It replaces
[GPT-6 Astra](/models/openai-gpt-6-astra), at **20% lower token prices**.
OpenAI skipped a public GPT-6.1 Astra: it pulled that release in late
September after safety concerns were raised.

- The hardest reasoning, coding and computer-use work in OpenAI's line-up.
- OpenAI says it scores 5.2 points higher than GPT-6 Astra on Agents' Last Exam
  while costing less than a third as much per task.
- For most everyday agentic coding,
  [GPT-6.2 Sol](/models/openai-gpt-6-2-sol) gets close at about one-fifth of
  the cost. Check Sol before paying for Astra.

OpenAI says GPT-6.2 Astra is its first externally released model to reach the
**High** threshold for AI self-improvement under its Preparedness Framework.
It ships with tighter safeguards on higher-risk cybersecurity requests.

## Specs & cost

- **Model ID:** `gpt-6.2-astra` — confirmed in OpenAI's own OpenAPI spec.
- **Context:** 1,050,000 tokens (922K max input), up to 128K output.
- **Modalities:** text and image in, text out.
- **Pricing:** $8 / 1M input, $40 / 1M output, $0.40 / 1M cached input. Above
  272K input tokens the whole request is billed at 2× input and 1.5× output.
- **Availability:** the OpenAI API, plus ChatGPT and Codex for Plus, Pro,
  Enterprise and Edu users.

> **The price and context figures are from launch coverage, not read directly
> from OpenAI.** OpenAI's docs and pricing pages could not be reached from the
> environment that wrote this page. Several independent reports agree on them,
> and the context window matches GPT-6 Astra's. Only the model ID was checked
> against an OpenAI source.

## Benchmark notes

**No benchmarks are recorded yet.** OpenAI's headline launch claims, Agents'
Last Exam and FrontierCode 2.0, are outside this catalog's tracked set. The
GPQA Diamond figures in circulation conflict: 96 and 97 are both attributed to
Astra, with no clear origin. Artificial Analysis gives it 62 on its
Intelligence Index at max effort, which is not a tracked benchmark.
