## When to reach for Kimi K3.1 Pro

Kimi K3.1 Pro (October 23, 2026) is Moonshot AI's new flagship — a post-trained
successor to [Kimi K3](/models/moonshot-kimi-k3) on the same ~2.8T-parameter
mixture-of-experts base (~104B active). It always thinks.

- **Cheaper than K3:** $2 / $10 per 1M against K3's $3 / $15.
- A big capability jump: Artificial Analysis scores it 55 on its Intelligence
  Index, 11 points above K3.
- A faster **Highspeed** variant shipped the same day at $4 / $20.

> **Weights not confirmed yet.** Moonshot said the full weights would be
> released by November 4, 2026; as of October 31 they were not out, and their
> release could not be confirmed for this update. It is listed as API-only until
> they are verified — for downloadable Kimi weights today, use
> [Kimi K3](/models/moonshot-kimi-k3).

## Specs & cost

- **Architecture:** sparse MoE, ~2.8T total parameters (Moonshot calls it
  "3T-class"), ~104B active per token.
- **Context:** 1,048,576 tokens.
- **Pricing:** $2 / 1M input, $0.20 / 1M cached input, $10 / 1M output.
  Third-party hosts list different rates.

## Benchmark notes

**No benchmarks are recorded.** A Terminal-Bench 4.0 score of 65.7 is reported
by one source and appears to come from Moonshot's own runs; it could not be
corroborated, so it is not used. Its Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index
score (55) is outside this catalog's tracked set.
