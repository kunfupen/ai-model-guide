## When to reach for DeepSeek V4-Flash

DeepSeek V4-Flash (April 24, 2026) is the small half of the V4 family — 284B
total parameters activating ~13B per token — and among the **cheapest credible
models anywhere** at $0.14 / $0.28 per million tokens.

- High-volume work where per-token cost is the binding constraint: classification,
  extraction, routing, bulk summarisation.
- Long-context jobs on a budget — it keeps the family's 1M-token window, which is
  unusual at this price.
- Self-hosting a capable model on modest hardware, under MIT.

Step up to **DeepSeek V4-Pro** when the task needs the extra capability.

## Specs & cost

- **Architecture:** 284B total parameters, MoE, ~13B active per token.
- **Context:** 1M tokens, up to 384K output.
- **Pricing:** $0.14 / 1M input and $0.28 / 1M output.
- **Licence:** MIT (open weights).

> Benchmark scores intentionally omitted — the published V4 figures are for the
> Pro variant, and carrying them across to Flash would misrepresent a materially
> smaller model.
