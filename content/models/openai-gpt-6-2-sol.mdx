## When to reach for GPT-6.2 Sol

GPT-6.2 Sol (November 17, 2026) is OpenAI's mid-priced GPT-6 model. It
replaces [GPT-6.1 Sol](/models/openai-gpt-6-1-sol) at the same price. OpenAI's
pitch is that it lands **within half a point of
[GPT-6.2 Astra](/models/openai-gpt-6-2-astra) on FrontierCode 2.0 at about
one-fifth of the cost**.

- Day-to-day agentic coding and computer use where Astra's extra headroom
  isn't worth a 4× token bill.
- Codex and ChatGPT users: Sol is the default everyday model. On the Pro plan
  it also gets OpenAI's Ultrafast mode.
- For lighter agent work, [GPT-6.1 Terra](/models/openai-gpt-6-1-terra) is
  half the price again.

## Specs & cost

- **Model ID:** `gpt-6.2-sol` — confirmed in OpenAI's own OpenAPI spec.
- **Context:** 1,050,000 tokens (922K max input), up to 128K output.
- **Pricing:** $2 / 1M input, $10 / 1M output, $0.10 / 1M cached input, the
  same as GPT-6.1 Sol. Above 272K input tokens it rises to $4 / $15.
- **Availability:** the OpenAI API, plus ChatGPT and Codex for Plus, Pro,
  Enterprise and Edu users.

> **The price and context figures are from launch coverage, not read directly
> from OpenAI.** OpenAI's docs and pricing pages could not be reached from the
> environment that wrote this page. Several independent reports agree on them.
> Only the model ID was checked against an OpenAI source.

## Benchmark notes

**No benchmarks are recorded yet.** OpenAI's headline FrontierCode 2.0 (43.8)
and BenchCAD 2.1 Core (73.1) figures are outside this catalog's tracked set.
Coverage of them is also inconsistent: one site gives FrontierCode 2.0 as 57
on a different scale. Vals AI ranks it #9 of 41 on the Vals Index (66.06),
which is not a tracked benchmark. Its Terminal-Bench 4.0 run could not be
read from here.
