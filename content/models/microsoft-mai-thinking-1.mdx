## When to reach for MAI-Thinking-1

MAI-Thinking-1 is the reasoning flagship of the **MAI** family Microsoft unveiled
at Build 2026 (June 2) — seven in-house models that mark its first serious move
away from depending solely on OpenAI. It is a sparse mixture-of-experts design
with roughly **35B active parameters out of ~1T total** and a 256K context window.

- Reasoning-heavy work inside the Microsoft/Azure ecosystem, where first-party
  models simplify procurement and data-residency questions.
- Teams that care about training provenance: Microsoft states the MAI models were
  trained on a licensed, "clean" dataset **with no distillation from third-party
  systems** — an unusual and checkable claim in this generation.
- Its stablemate **MAI-Code-1-Flash** (5B) ships in GitHub Copilot and reaches 51%
  on SWE-bench Pro, which is notable for that size.

## Specs

- **Architecture:** sparse MoE, ~1T total parameters, ~35B active per token.
- **Context:** 256K tokens.
- **Availability:** Azure AI Foundry and Microsoft's own surfaces (Copilot).

> Pricing is not published in a comparable per-token form, so it is omitted rather
> than estimated.

## Benchmark notes

Microsoft reports **97.0% on AIME 2025** and describes MAI-Thinking-1 as
competitive with Claude Opus 4.6 on **SWE-bench Pro**. Neither is recorded here:
AIME is outside this catalog's tracked set, and SWE-bench Pro is a different,
harder benchmark than the SWE-bench **Verified** column used elsewhere — no
standalone Verified figure was published.
