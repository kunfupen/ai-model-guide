## When to reach for Gemini 4 Argon

Gemini 4 Argon is the first model of Google's **Gemini 4** generation — a
reasoning model for long, multi-step work such as software engineering, legal
and financial analysis, and cyber defence. Google announced it on September 30,
2026 for its Fairwind cyber-defender program first; it became **generally
available on the paid Gemini API on October 13**, which is the date used here.

- Long agentic tasks over large inputs: one 1M-token window shared by input,
  thinking and output.
- Very long generations: with long decode, a single response can run close to
  1M tokens.
- Wide multimodal input — text, image, video, audio and PDF.

Independent testing puts it roughly level with
[GPT-6 Astra](/models/openai-gpt-6-astra).

## Specs & cost

- **Model ID:** `gemini-4-argon`.
- **Context:** 1,048,576 tokens, shared between input, thinking and output.
  Default output cap is 65,536 tokens; long decode lifts it towards the full
  window.
- **Pricing:** listed at the standard **$4 / $20 per 1M**. Google is charging an
  introductory **$2 / $10 through November 30, 2026**; cached input is 95% off at
  both rates, and output pricing includes thinking tokens.
- **Availability:** paid Gemini API tier only — not on the free tier.
- **Knowledge cutoff:** reported as March 2026 by third parties; not confirmed
  on Google's own page.

## Benchmark notes

**No benchmarks are recorded.** A Terminal-Bench 4.0 figure of 57.1 circulates
on comparison sites, but its origin is unclear — the same sites list a GPT-6.1
Sol figure that matches neither OpenAI's nor Vals AI's — so it is not used.

## Gemini 4 Carbon

Google announced **Gemini 4 Carbon**, a stronger model, on October 28. It is
limited to selected cyber defenders, with no public model ID, model card or
release date, so it is not listed.
