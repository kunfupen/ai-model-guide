## When to reach for DeepSeek V4.1 Flash

DeepSeek V4.1 Flash (September 10, 2026) is DeepSeek's current main model, and
on the Vals Index the **strongest open-weight model available** — narrowly
ahead of [Kimi K3](/models/moonshot-kimi-k3) at roughly one-twentieth the cost
per test.

- Open-weight deployment where you want frontier-adjacent quality.
- Bulk work priced near the floor of the market, especially off-peak.
- Image-in workloads: native image input is new in this generation.

> **It has quietly replaced V4 Flash.** The legacy IDs `deepseek-v4-flash` and
> `deepseek-v4-flash-vision-exp` are still accepted, but **every request to them
> is now served by V4.1 Flash and billed at V4.1 Flash's prices.** If you pinned
> V4 Flash, you are already on this model.

## Specs & cost

- **Model ID:** `deepseek-flash` going forward.
- **Architecture:** 552B-parameter mixture-of-experts on DeepSeek's new causal
  encoder–decoder design, with ~8B parameters active for input and ~16B for
  output.
- **Context:** 1M tokens.
- **Pricing:** **$0.30 / $1.20 per 1M at peak**, which is what is recorded here
  as the standard rate. Off-peak is half that — $0.15 / $0.60, with cache hits at
  $0.003. Peak hours are 01:00–04:00 and 06:00–10:00 UTC on weekdays, so most of
  the week is off-peak.

## Benchmark notes

**No benchmarks are recorded.** Its headline independent result is the Vals
Index (57.86), which is outside this catalog's tracked set. DeepSeek says it
beats both V4 Flash and the V4 Pro tier, but published no figure on a tracked
benchmark.
