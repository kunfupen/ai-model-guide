## When to reach for Kimi K2 Thinking

Kimi K2 Thinking (November 6, 2025) is the reasoning and agentic sibling of
Kimi K2, with separately released weights and its own model card — a distinct
release, not an effort suffix. Moonshot's own K2 repo describes K2-Instruct as
"a reflex-grade model without long thinking"; this is the model that thinks.

- Very long tool-use chains: it sustains **200–300 sequential tool calls**
  without human intervention.
- Native INT4 quantisation, which makes a 1T-parameter model far more tractable
  to host.

> Superseded by **[Kimi K2.5](/models/moonshot-kimi-k2-5)** onward. Listed for
> historical depth between Kimi K2 (July 2025) and the K2.5 multimodal turn.

## Specs & licence

- **Architecture:** 1T total / 32B activated MoE, native INT4.
- **Context:** 262,144 tokens.
- **Licence:** Modified MIT. Weights at `moonshotai/Kimi-K2-Thinking`.

## Benchmark notes

**No benchmarks are recorded.** The widely cited Humanity's Last Exam score of
44.9 is a **with-tools vendor figure**, and the independent HLE leaderboard
placed the model far lower (~23.9) within days of launch. No score for this
model met this catalog's bar of a URL that attests the figure, so none is shown
rather than showing one that would mislead.
