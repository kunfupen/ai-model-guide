## When to reach for Grok 4.7

Grok 4.7 (September 21, 2026) is xAI's new flagship, which it calls its most
capable model yet for coding and knowledge work. The design change is
behavioural: it spends longer on hard problems and **double-checks its own
answers more often** than Grok 4.6 — the issue Musk cited when he delayed the
launch, saying the model was stopping difficult tasks too early.

- **Same price as 4.6.** $2 / $6 per 1M is unchanged, which makes it one of
  the cheapest frontier-adjacent models in this catalog.
- Long-running coding agents: CursorBench 4.0 rose to 46.3 from 40.4, and
  DeepSWE v1.1 to 71.0 from 65.2 (high effort).
- Legal and professional work, where xAI reports a large lead on Harvey's
  Legal Agent Benchmark.

It is **not a frontier leader.** On independent measurement it scores 46 on the
Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, well behind
[Claude Opus 5.5](/models/anthropic-claude-opus-5-5) at 58, and on Terminal-Bench
4.0 it sits far below Opus 5.5 (66.4) and
[GPT-6 Astra](/models/openai-gpt-6-astra) (57.7). The case for it is price.

## Specs & cost

- **Model ID:** `grok-4.7`.
- **Context:** 500K tokens; text and image in, text out, no text output limit.
- **Pricing:** $2 / 1M input, $6 / 1M output.
- **Availability:** xAI API, Grok Build, Cursor, and GitHub Copilot (rolling
  out from September 21), plus third-party coding harnesses and clouds.

## Benchmark notes

**Terminal-Bench 4.0 (38.0)** is recorded — xAI's headline figure, run on
SpaceXAI's **internal harness**.

**Be aware xAI published two different Terminal-Bench 4.0 numbers.** The 38.0
comes from its internal harness; the same benchmark run **through Grok Build**
scored **33**. Same model, same benchmark, five points apart purely from the
harness. The headline figure is recorded here, but the gap is a useful reminder
that every Terminal-Bench 4.0 row in this catalog is vendor-run on the vendor's
own setup.

**No SWE-bench Verified, GPQA Diamond or Terminal-Bench 2.1 score is
recorded** — xAI published none of them. Its coding claims rest on CursorBench
4.0 (46.3), DeepSWE v1.1 (71.0) and EEBench (66.0), none of which are in the
tracked set.

## Release history

Grok 4.7 slipped repeatedly before shipping. Musk said "10 days" on September 2,
then that it "needs a few more days to cook" on September 11; on September 13 he
announced a larger **Grok 4.8** (2.5T parameters), which briefly looked like it
had superseded 4.7 entirely. 4.7 then shipped on September 21. Grok 4.8 has no
release date, price or specs, and is not listed.
