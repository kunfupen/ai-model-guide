## When to reach for Gemini 3.8 Flash

Gemini 3.8 Flash (September 2, 2026) is Google's most capable Flash-tier model
and its **third Flash release in six weeks**. Google positions it for
long-horizon software engineering, autonomous agents, and enterprise workflows.

- It posts **90.8 on Terminal-Bench 2.1 — the highest in this catalog**, ahead
  of GPT-5.6 Sol (88.8) and Kimi K3 (88.3), at a fraction of their price.
- Agentic coding on a budget: $0.75 / $3.75 during the introductory period,
  $1.50 / $7.50 after.
- Wide multimodal input — text, images, video, audio and PDFs in a single
  request.

> **Listed at the standard rate.** The frontmatter price is the post-introductory
> **$1.50 / $7.50**, matching how
> [Gemini 3.7 Flash](/models/google-gemini-3-7-flash) is recorded, so the
> comparison table stays apples-to-apples. Both models are currently discounted
> to **$0.75 / $3.75 through December 31, 2026**; the rate doubles on January 1.

## Specs & cost

- **Model ID:** `gemini-3.8-flash`.
- **Context:** 1,048,576 tokens in, 65,536 tokens out.
- **Inputs:** text, image, video, audio and PDF; text output.
- **Pricing:** $0.75 / $3.75 per 1M through 2026-12-31, then $1.50 / $7.50.
- **Availability:** Gemini API, Google AI Studio, Antigravity, Android Studio
  and Gemini Enterprise.

## Benchmark notes

Terminal-Bench 2.1 (90.8) and Terminal-Bench 4.0 (19.1) are recorded, both from
Google's own launch comparison.

**A caveat on comparing the Terminal-Bench 2.1 column.** Google's comparison puts
Gemini 3.7 Flash at **81.6**, while this catalog's 3.7 Flash row records **85.8**
from Artificial Analysis's independent run. Same benchmark name, different
harnesses — so reading 90.8 against 85.8 as a "+5.0 improvement" mixes two
measurement sources. Against Google's own baseline the jump is +9.2. The 3.7
Flash row is left as measured rather than quietly restated.

Terminal-Bench **4.0** is a different and much harder benchmark than **2.1** —
the 19.1 and 90.8 figures are not comparable to each other. Google also
published OSWorld-2.0 (59.0), GDPVal-AA v2 (1545 Elo) and BioMysteryBench, none
of which are in this catalog's tracked set, so they are not recorded. On the
hardest agentic rows Opus 5 still leads clearly — Terminal-Bench 4.0 51.8 vs
19.1, OSWorld-2.0 75.4 vs 59.0.

## Gemini 3.8 Flash Cyber

A companion release shares the same underlying model but ships more permissive
cybersecurity mitigations. It is **not openly deployable** — access is granted
case by case through Google's Fairwind Program to trusted government
authorities, critical infrastructure operators and software maintainers. Since
it cannot be signed up for, it is not listed separately here.
