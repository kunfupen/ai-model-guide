## When to reach for Qwen4-Max

Qwen4-Max (November 16, 2026) is the first model of Alibaba's Qwen 4 series and
its new flagship. It replaces [Qwen3.8-Max](/models/alibaba-qwen-3-8-max). It
became generally available after a limited preview that started on
November 11.

- Alibaba's strongest hosted model for reasoning and coding, with a context
  window of up to one million tokens.
- Workloads where token efficiency matters: Alibaba says it uses fewer tokens
  than Qwen3.8-Max on comparable tasks.
- Image input is built in.

It is **not open weights**. Alibaba says smaller open-weight Qwen 4 models will
follow, with no date given. Until then,
[Qwen3.8-27B](/models/alibaba-qwen-3-8-27b) is the run-it-yourself option.

## Specs & cost

- **Architecture:** mixture-of-experts, 2.4 trillion total parameters.
- **Context:** up to 1,000,000 tokens.
- **Availability:** QwenCloud, plus Alibaba's QwenWork and QwenStudio.
- **Pricing:** not recorded. Launch coverage quotes $1.50 / $4.50 per 1M tokens,
  but at least one report says that figure is unconfirmed, so it is left out
  until Alibaba's own price list can be checked.

> **These specs come from launch coverage of Alibaba's announcement.** Alibaba's
> own pages could not be checked from the environment that wrote this page, and
> the API model ID is not recorded for the same reason.
