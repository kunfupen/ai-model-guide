## When to reach for GPT-6 Astra

GPT-6 Astra (September 3, 2026) is OpenAI's new flagship and the first model of
the GPT-6 generation. OpenAI positions it for the hardest end-to-end work —
complex reasoning, coding, research, document creation — and calls it **"the
world's best computer use model"**, built to drive browsers, spreadsheets and
desktop applications rather than just emit text.

- It takes the top **GPQA Diamond** score in this catalog at **96.0**, ahead of
  Claude Fable 5 (95.0) and Claude Mythos 5 (95.0).
- It leads **Terminal-Bench 4.0** at 57.7, just ahead of
  [Claude Fable 5.1](/models/anthropic-claude-fable-5-1) (55.8).
- Computer-use and desktop agent work, where OSWorld 2.0 (72.6) is its
  strongest published signal.

## Specs & cost

- **Model ID:** `gpt-6-astra` — confirmed in OpenAI's own OpenAPI spec.
- **Context:** 1,050,000 tokens, up to 128K output.
- **Modalities:** text and image in, text out. No native audio input.
- **Knowledge cutoff:** April 30, 2026.
- **Pricing:** $10 / 1M input, $50 / 1M output — the same rate as
  [Claude Fable 5.1](/models/anthropic-claude-fable-5-1).
- **Availability:** ChatGPT Plus, Pro, Business and Enterprise; the OpenAI API;
  and Amazon Bedrock. Rollout began with enterprise customers in OpenAI's gated
  **Daybreak** program, so access may lag the announcement depending on tier.

## Benchmark notes

Three figures are recorded: GPQA Diamond (96.0), Terminal-Bench 4.0 (57.7) and
Terminal-Bench-Science 0.1 (64.6). All are **OpenAI-reported launch numbers**
with no independent replication yet.

**No SWE-bench Verified score is recorded.** OpenAI did not publish one for
Astra. Its software-engineering claim rests on **DeepSWE v1.1 (74.1)**, which is
a different benchmark and not part of this catalog's tracked set — so it is not
substituted into the SWE-bench column. On that column
[GPT-5.6 Sol](/models/openai-gpt-5-6-sol) remains OpenAI's measured entry here.

Also published and **not** recorded, being outside the tracked set: FrontierMath
Tier 4 v2 (97.6), BenchCAD (95.9), OSWorld 2.0 (72.6), ARC-AGI-3 (99.9) and
ExploitBench (100).

> **Read the cyber numbers carefully.** OpenAI states Astra crossed its
> **Critical cyber-capability threshold**, that release was delayed for
> safeguard work, and that the headline cyber evaluation ran on **Daybreak
> Blue rather than the default production configuration**. The perfect
> ExploitBench score therefore does not describe the model as most people will
> call it. It is not recorded here.
>
> The ARC-AGI-3 result (99.9) was likewise measured under OpenAI's own provider
> adapter harness.
