## When to reach for Gemini 3.7 Flash

Gemini 3.7 Flash (GA August 13, 2026) is Google's self-described "most intelligent
workhorse model yet for coding and agents" — a substantial jump over 3.6 Flash on
software engineering, web development, and agentic workflows rather than a routine
refresh.

- Agentic coding at workhorse prices: **85.8 on Terminal-Bench 2.1** puts it above
  every Claude and Gemini model in this catalog and behind only GPT-5.6 Sol (88.8)
  and Grok 4.6 (88.4) — both of which cost considerably more.
- Long-horizon software engineering: 65.3% on DeepSWE v1.1, against 48.6% for
  3.6 Flash.
- Long-context retrieval: 97.0% on GDM-MRCR v2 at 128k, effectively near-perfect.
- High-volume multimodal work — text, image, video, audio and PDF in.

## Specs & cost

- **Context:** 1,048,576 input tokens, up to 65,536 output.
- **Pricing:** $1.50 / 1M input and $7.50 / 1M output at standard rates — with
  **introductory pricing of $0.75 / $3.75 through December 31, 2026**. Batch is
  roughly a quarter of list ($0.1875 / $0.9375).
- **Availability:** Gemini API / AI Studio, the Gemini app, and Vertex AI.

## Notes

Google shipped this while **Gemini 3.5 Pro remains delayed**, so the Flash line is
currently carrying Google's frontier story. At standard rates 3.7 Flash lists the
same as 3.6 Flash, so there is little reason to stay on 3.6; if raw cost dominates,
**Gemini 3.5 Flash** is still the cheaper tier at $0.30/$2.50.

## Benchmark notes

Terminal-Bench 2.1 (85.8) is from Artificial Analysis, the same harness used for
the other agentic-coding scores here. DeepSWE, GDM-MRCR and the Artificial
Analysis Intelligence Index are cited above as context but are **not** in this
catalog's tracked set, so they are not recorded as benchmark entries. GPQA Diamond
is omitted — it appears in the index Google reports against, but no standalone
figure for this model was published.
