## When to reach for Qwen3.8-27B

Qwen3.8-27B (August 14, 2026) is the open-weight member of the Qwen3.8 generation:
a 27-billion-parameter multimodal model released under the permissive **Apache 2.0**
licence, which makes it one of the more consequential local-AI releases of 2026.

- Self-hosting, privacy, and on-prem deployment with no usage restrictions worth
  worrying about — Apache 2.0 is genuinely permissive, unlike several "open" LLM
  licences.
- Fine-tuning on your own data, where 27B is large enough to be capable and small
  enough to train affordably.
- Multimodal work locally: text and image input.

For frontier quality, **Qwen3.8-Max** is the hosted sibling; this is the run-it-yourself option.

## Specs

- **Parameters:** ~27B.
- **Context:** 256K tokens.
- **Licence:** Apache 2.0 (open weights).
- **Availability:** downloadable weights, plus hosted options from several providers.
