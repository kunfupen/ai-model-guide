## When to reach for Grok 4.5

Grok 4.5 is xAI's flagship (developer launch July 8, 2026; public July 9). Musk
pitched it as **"an Opus-class model, but faster, more token-efficient and lower
cost"** — and independent testing broadly backs the price/performance claim:
Artificial Analysis ranked it #4 of 168 models on its Intelligence Index, near
the frontier at a fraction of rivals' cost per task.

- Strong general reasoning and agentic coding at an aggressive price point.
- Workloads already integrated with X / the xAI ecosystem.
- Cost-sensitive frontier work — it undercuts most Opus/GPT-flagship pricing.

## Specs & cost

- **Context:** 500K tokens.
- **Pricing:** $2 / 1M input and $6 / 1M output (cached input $0.50, a 75%
  discount).
- **Availability:** xAI API (and the Grok apps).

## Benchmark notes

SWE-bench Verified (86.6), GPQA Diamond (93.1), and Terminal-Bench 2.1 (83.3)
are from the same independent leaderboards used across this catalog (Vals AI /
Artificial Analysis / BenchLM). Note xAI's *own* launch materials omitted GPQA
and other pure-reasoning evals, so these third-party numbers give a fuller
picture. MMLU / HumanEval not reported in comparable form.
