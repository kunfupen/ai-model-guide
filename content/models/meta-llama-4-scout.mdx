## When to reach for Llama 4 Scout

Scout is the smaller sibling to Llama 4 Maverick, released the same day
(April 5, 2025): 109B total parameters with 17B active across 16 experts,
natively multimodal, and carrying a claimed **10M-token context window** that
was an industry first at the time.

> **Historical.** Meta has since abandoned the Llama line in favour of the Muse
> family — see **[Muse Spark 1.2](/models/meta-muse-spark-1-2)** for the current
> flagship and **[Muse Glimmer](/models/meta-muse-glimmer)** for the current
> open-weight option. Scout is listed for completeness and for anyone still
> running it.

## Specs & licence

- **Architecture:** 109B total / 17B active, 16 experts, MoE.
- **Context:** 10M tokens as claimed. In practice this was widely reported as
  difficult to reproduce — long-context quality degrades well below the headline
  number, so treat it as a ceiling, not a working figure.
- **Licence:** Llama 4 Community License (not OSI-approved). Weights at
  `meta-llama/Llama-4-Scout-17B-16E-Instruct`.

## Benchmark notes

**No benchmarks are recorded.** The vendor-reported Llama 4 numbers were
disputed within days of launch amid the LMArena and benchmark-contamination
controversy, and no figure from that period cleared this catalog's sourcing bar.
