## When to reach for Grok 4.6

Grok 4.6 (released August 12, 2026) is xAI's frontier model for coding, agentic
tasks, and knowledge work — a direct successor to Grok 4.5 at **the same price**.
Independent coverage puts it at rough parity with Claude Fable 5 and GPT-5.6 Sol,
and it currently posts the **highest published GPQA Diamond score** of any model
in this catalog.

- Frontier reasoning and agentic coding at a notably low price point.
- Long-context work, with the caveat that pricing doubles past 200K tokens.
- Teams already on the xAI API, Cursor, OpenRouter, Vercel, or Cloudflare.

## Specs & cost

- **Context:** 500K tokens.
- **Pricing:** $2 / 1M input and $6 / 1M output for prompts under 200K tokens;
  beyond that the long-context band doubles it to $4 / $12.
- **Scale:** described by Musk as a ~1.5-trillion-parameter model with heavily
  revised supervised fine-tuning and RL.
- **Availability:** xAI API, Grok Build, Cursor, OpenRouter, Vercel, Cloudflare.

## Benchmark notes

GPQA Diamond (94.9, "high" effort) and Terminal-Bench 2.1 (88.4, high effort) are
from Artificial Analysis. **SWE-bench Verified is omitted** — no score for 4.6 was
published at the time of writing (86.6 belongs to Grok 4.5 and must not be carried
over). Note both figures come from a high-effort configuration, so they sit at the
top of this model's range rather than at its default settings.
