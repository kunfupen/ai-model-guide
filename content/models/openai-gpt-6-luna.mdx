## When to reach for GPT-6 Luna

GPT-6 Luna (September 22, 2026) is the fast, cost-efficient tier of OpenAI's
GPT-6 series, positioned below [GPT-6.1 Sol](/models/openai-gpt-6-1-sol) and
built for high-volume, latency-sensitive work.

- Classification, extraction, routing and other jobs run millions of times.
- The first hop of an agent pipeline, escalating to Sol or Astra only when
  needed.
- At **$0.10 / $0.50 per 1M** it is the cheapest model in this catalog from any
  of the major US labs.

## Specs & cost

- **Model ID:** `gpt-6-luna` — confirmed in OpenAI's own OpenAPI spec.
- **Context:** 1,050,000 tokens, up to 128K output.
- **Knowledge cutoff:** May 18, 2026 — later than Sol's April 30.
- **Pricing:** $0.10 / 1M input, $0.50 / 1M output, $0.01 / 1M cached input.
  **Requests above 272K input tokens are billed at $0.20 / $0.75** — worth
  knowing before you lean on the full context window.
- **Availability:** the OpenAI API, ChatGPT and Codex, and Microsoft Foundry.

## Benchmark notes

**No benchmarks are recorded.** OpenAI says Luna outperforms its predecessor on
its own tests but published no figure on a benchmark this catalog tracks.
