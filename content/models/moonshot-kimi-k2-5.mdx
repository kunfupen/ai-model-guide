## When to reach for Kimi K2.5

Kimi K2.5 (January 27, 2026) is the **first natively multimodal model in the Kimi
line** — continued pretraining on roughly 15T mixed vision+text tokens made
image and video first-class inputs rather than a bolted-on adapter.

- Multimodal work you want to self-host, under a Modified MIT licence.
- Available on **Amazon Bedrock**, which is useful independent confirmation of
  general availability rather than a preview drop.

> Superseded by **[Kimi K2.6](/models/moonshot-kimi-k2-6)** and then
> **[Kimi K3](/models/moonshot-kimi-k3)**.

## Specs & licence

- **Architecture:** 1T total / 32B activated MoE.
- **Context:** 262,144 tokens.
- **Modalities:** text, image and video in. Video chat was API-only at launch.
- **Licence:** Modified MIT. Weights at `moonshotai/Kimi-K2.5`.
- **Date:** January 27 comes from secondary coverage; the official GitHub repo
  was created January 30, so the date is accurate to within a few days.

## Benchmark notes

SWE-bench Verified (76.8) and GPQA Diamond (87.6) are recorded. Both are from
Moonshot's own README and so are vendor-reported.

Moonshot also published **MMLU-Pro (87.1)** and **MMMU-Pro (78.5)**. MMLU-Pro is
a different benchmark from the MMLU column used elsewhere in this catalog and is
not a substitute for it, so neither is recorded.
