## When to reach for GLM-5.2

GLM-5.2 (June 13, 2026) is Zhipu's open-weight coding model and, on standard
coding benchmarks, the **strongest open-weights model available** — a 753B
mixture-of-experts design activating ~40B parameters per token, released under a
plain **MIT licence** with no regional restrictions.

- Coding and agentic work at a fraction of frontier pricing ($1.40/$4.40).
- It currently **leads τ²-bench Telecom (99.1)** in this catalog — ahead of every
  proprietary model here.
- Self-hosting without licence friction: MIT is about as permissive as it gets,
  and the weights are on Hugging Face (`zai-org/GLM-5.2`).
- 1M-token context with up to 128K output.

> Superseded as Z.ai's flagship by **[GLM-5.3](/models/zhipu-glm-5-3)** (August
> 2026) — but 5.3 launched Coding-Plan-only with no published weights, so **5.2
> is still the current MIT-licensed GLM** and the right pick if you need to
> self-host.

## Specs & cost

- **Architecture:** 753B total parameters, MoE, ~40B active per token.
- **Context:** 1M tokens, up to ~131K output.
- **Pricing:** $1.40 / 1M input and $4.40 / 1M output. A GLM Coding Plan
  subscription starts at $18/month.
- **Licence:** MIT (open weights).

## Benchmark notes

Terminal-Bench 2.1 (81.0) and τ²-bench Telecom (99.1) are recorded.

**SWE-bench Verified is omitted.** Zhipu published SWE-bench **Pro** (62.1) — a
different, harder benchmark that is not interchangeable with the Verified column
used elsewhere here. AIME 2026 (99.2) is vendor-reported and outside the tracked
set, so it is not recorded either.
