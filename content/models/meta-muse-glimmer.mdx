## When to reach for Muse Glimmer

Muse Glimmer (August 10, 2026) is Meta's return to open weights and its **first
model under Apache 2.0** — the Llama line shipped under Meta's own community
licence, which carried usage restrictions Apache does not. At ~29.6B parameters
it is built to run agentic workloads on a single consumer GPU.

- Local and on-device agents where a 1T-parameter model is not an option.
- Projects that need a permissive licence with no acceptable-use rider or
  monthly-active-user clause.
- A distillation of Muse Spark, so it inherits the flagship's agentic behaviour
  at a fraction of the footprint.

## Specs & licence

- **Architecture:** ~29.6B total — a ~1.8B ViT-G/14 perception encoder plus a
  ~28B dense text decoder. Dense, not MoE.
- **Context:** 131,072 tokens per the model card, vLLM recipes and independent
  write-ups. Meta's own launch blog says 32,768; the model-card figure is used
  here, but the discrepancy is unresolved.
- **Modalities:** interleaved text + image in, text out. Audio is explicitly out
  of scope; some coverage mentions video input, which the model card does not
  confirm.
- **Licence:** Apache 2.0. Weights at `meta-models/Muse-Glimmer-30B` on Hugging
  Face.

## Benchmark notes

**No benchmarks are recorded.** Meta published SWE-Bench **Pro** (51.2), AIME
2026 (94.7) and MCP Atlas (75.5), and Artificial Analysis measured an
Intelligence Index of 35 — none of which belong to this catalog's tracked set.
SWE-bench Pro in particular is a different, harder benchmark than the SWE-bench
Verified column used elsewhere here, and the two are not interchangeable.
