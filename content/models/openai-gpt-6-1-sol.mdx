## When to reach for GPT-6.1 Sol

GPT-6.1 Sol (September 29, 2026) is OpenAI's mid-priced GPT-6 model. OpenAI
says it **nearly matches [GPT-6 Astra](/models/openai-gpt-6-astra) on agentic
coding, computer use and professional work at one-fifth of Astra's token
prices** — and the launch data mostly backs that up.

- Agentic coding: 75.2% on DeepSWE v1.1 at high effort for about **$0.65 per
  task**, against Astra's 74.1% for $4.43.
- Computer use: 71.4% on OSWorld 2.0 for $1.27 per task, against Astra's 73.5%
  for $9.44.
- Anything you were going to run on Astra where the last two points don't
  justify a 5× bill.

It replaced **GPT-6 Sol**, which shipped on September 22 at the same price and
was superseded a week later. That model is not listed separately.

## Specs & cost

- **Model ID:** `gpt-6.1-sol` — confirmed in OpenAI's own OpenAPI spec.
- **Context:** 1,050,000 tokens, up to 128K output.
- **Knowledge cutoff:** April 30, 2026.
- **Pricing:** $2 / 1M input, $10 / 1M output, $0.10 / 1M cached input.
- **Availability:** the OpenAI API, ChatGPT and Codex.

## Benchmark notes

**Terminal-Bench 4.0 (55.05)** is recorded from **Vals AI's independent run**
(±1.82). That is a different kind of source from the other rows in that column,
which are all vendor-run on each lab's own harness — so read small gaps there
with care. A figure of 56.1 also circulates for 6.1 Sol with no clear origin;
it is not used.

**No SWE-bench Verified or GPQA Diamond score is recorded** — none was
published. DeepSWE v1.1 and OSWorld 2.0, OpenAI's headline numbers, are outside
this catalog's tracked set.
