## When to reach for Mistral Large 4

Mistral Large 4 (nicknamed "Le Chonk") is Mistral AI's flagship: a
**1-trillion-parameter**, natively multimodal mixture-of-experts model. It went
into public API preview on October 6, 2026, and Mistral released an improved
version **with open weights on October 29** — the date used here.

- The strongest open-weight model from **outside China**, per one Artificial
  Analysis-based comparison (leading Chinese open models still score higher).
- Coding and **defensive security** work, where Mistral claims it leads open
  models.
- Very low API pricing for its size: $0.70 / $2.10 per 1M.
- A European option for teams that prefer an EU-based provider.

## Specs & cost

- **Architecture:** 1T total parameters, MoE. Active parameters are reported as
  ~52B (also seen as 49B); not confirmed here.
- **Context:** 1M tokens.
- **Pricing:** $0.70 / 1M input, $0.07 / 1M cached input, $2.10 / 1M output,
  per Mistral's announcement. Pre-release pages list $1.36 / $4.18 — outdated.
- **Licence:** the **Mistral Ecosystem Open-Weight License**, a modified MIT
  licence. Businesses offering the model to third parties as a hosted service
  need a separate commercial licence.

## Benchmark notes

**No benchmarks are recorded.** Mistral did not publish a full benchmark table
that could be checked, and the third-party figures in circulation are not
consistent enough to quote.
