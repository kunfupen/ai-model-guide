## When to reach for GLM-4.6V

GLM-4.6V (December 8, 2025) is the open-weight flagship of the GLM-V
vision-language line, and the first GLM vision model with **native multimodal
function calling** — tools can both consume and return images, video frames and
document pages, rather than passing text descriptions back and forth.

- Document and screen understanding pipelines where the tool loop itself needs
  to be visual.
- 128K context across text, image and video, MIT-licensed.

> Shipped alongside **GLM-4.6V-Flash** (9B, same licence, tuned for local and
> low-latency use). That is a separate model, not a config variant, and is not
> listed here.

## Specs & licence

- **Architecture:** 106B parameters.
- **Context:** 128K tokens.
- **Modalities:** text, image and video in.
- **Licence:** MIT.
