## When to reach for Gemini 3.6 Flash

Gemini 3.6 Flash (launched July 21, 2026) is the latest in Google's fast, cheap
Flash line, tuned for efficiency, latency, and reliability when building AI agents
at scale. It's a straightforward upgrade over Gemini 3.5 Flash across the board.

- High-volume, latency-sensitive agentic workloads at scale.
- Token-efficient pipelines: it uses ~17% fewer output tokens than 3.5 Flash on
  the Artificial Analysis Index. Note this offsets — but does not overcome — a
  higher list price: 3.6 Flash is $1.50/$7.50 against 3.5 Flash's $0.30/$2.50, so
  if raw cost is the priority, 3.5 Flash remains the cheaper tier.
- Long, multimodal inputs — text, image, video, audio, and PDF — with a full
  ~1M-token context window and sharply better long-context retrieval (91.8% on
  GDM-MRCR v2 at 128k).

For frontier reasoning, step up to a Pro-tier Gemini; Flash is the speed/cost pick.

## Specs & cost

- **Context:** 1,048,576 input tokens, up to 65,536 output.
- **Modalities:** text, image, video, audio, PDF in; text out.
- **Pricing:** $1.50 / 1M input and $7.50 / 1M output ($0.15 cached input).
- **Availability:** Google AI Studio, Gemini API, the Gemini app, Android Studio,
  Antigravity, and Vertex AI / Gemini Enterprise (day one).

## Benchmark notes

Google reports gains over 3.5 Flash on DeepSWE (49% vs 37%), OSWorld-Verified
(83.0 vs 78.4), and MLE-Bench (63.9 vs 49.7) — but not on our tracked
benchmarks (MMLU / HumanEval / SWE-bench Verified / GPQA Diamond) in comparable
form, so scores are omitted rather than mixed across incompatible suites.
