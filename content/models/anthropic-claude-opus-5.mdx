## When to reach for Claude Opus 5

Claude Opus 5 (released July 24, 2026) is the flagship of Anthropic's Claude 5
generation. Its headline pitch: performance approaching — and on several
benchmarks surpassing — the more expensive Fable 5, while keeping the **same API
pricing as Opus 4.8** ($5/$25). Anthropic frames it as frontier capability with a
"dial on your bill."

- The hardest agentic coding and long-horizon reasoning, now at a materially
  better price/performance than Fable 5 for most tasks.
- Enterprise and coding-agent workloads that need frontier quality without the
  top-tier token cost.
- Work that benefits from the 1M-token context window (128K max output).

## What's new vs. Opus 4.8

- **Big capability jump at the same price.** On Frontier-Bench v0.1 it more than
  doubles Opus 4.8's score; on CursorBench 3.2 (max effort) it lands within 0.5%
  of Fable 5 at half the cost per task; on OSWorld 2.0 (computer use) it beats
  Fable 5's best result at ~a third of the cost.
- **Leads SWE-bench Verified** at 97.0 — ahead of GPT-5.6 Sol (96.2) and Fable 5 (95.0).
- **Fast Mode** — 2.5× faster for 2× the price.

## Specs & cost

- **Context:** 1M tokens, 128K max output.
- **Pricing:** $5 / 1M input and $25 / 1M output — unchanged from Opus 4.8.
- **Availability:** Claude API/Platform, claude.ai, Amazon Bedrock, Vertex AI,
  and Microsoft Foundry.

> Benchmark notes: SWE-bench Verified (97.0) is corroborated by the Vals AI
> leaderboard. GPQA Diamond is saturated (~93–94% across top models, differences
> statistically insignificant) and omitted pending a stable published figure;
> MMLU / HumanEval not published.
