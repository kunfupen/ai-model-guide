## When to reach for GLM-5.3-Flash

GLM-5.3-Flash (August 26, 2026) is the small, cheap member of Z.ai's GLM-5.3
family — and, unusually, it ships under a **plain, unmodified MIT licence**,
while its larger sibling [GLM-5.3](/models/zhipu-glm-5-3) does not.

- Self-hosting a capable multimodal model with no licence strings attached.
- High-volume work at $0.15 / $0.50 per 1M.
- Deployments where the GLM-5.3 licence's revenue condition is a problem.

## Specs & cost

- **Architecture:** 320B-parameter mixture-of-experts, ~18B active per token.
- **Context:** 1M tokens per Artificial Analysis and DeepInfra; OpenRouter
  lists 1,310,720. The lower figure is recorded.
- **Modalities:** text and image in, text out. Some listings also mention
  video, which is not corroborated.
- **Pricing:** $0.15 / 1M input, $0.50 / 1M output at Z.ai's standard rate.
  Third-party hosts range from roughly half that to double.
- **Licence:** MIT.
