## When to reach for Gemini 4 Carbon

Gemini 4 Carbon is Google DeepMind's strongest model and the second of the
Gemini 4 generation, above [Gemini 4 Argon](/models/google-gemini-4-argon).
Google announced it on October 28 for selected cyber defenders; it became
**generally available on November 10, 2026** to every paying developer on the
Gemini API, which is the date used here.

- The hardest long-horizon reasoning and agentic work in the Gemini line.
- Agent systems built on Google's Antigravity platform, where Carbon launched
  with a preview "swarm" feature for coordinating many agents.
- Consumers: it is rolling out to Google AI Ultra subscribers in the Gemini app,
  Antigravity and AI Mode in Search. Argon stays available from AI Pro up.

## Specs & cost

- **Model ID:** reported as `gemini-4-carbon`, following Argon's naming.
- **Context:** 1,048,576 tokens, with output up to the full window.
- **Inputs:** text, image and video, with text output. Two of three listings
  also report audio and file input.
- **Pricing:** listed at the standard **$4 / $20 per 1M**. The introductory rate
  is **$2 / $10**, the same as Argon's. Carbon is **not on the free tier**, and it
  uses more reasoning tokens per task — billed as output — so matching Argon's
  per-token price does not mean matching its per-task cost.

> **The context window and input types come from third parties.** Google's launch
> post doesn't state them, and its model card could not be read from here. The
> figures above are where Artificial Analysis, Vals AI and OpenRouter all agree;
> they match Argon. The model ID is likewise reported rather than read from
> Google's docs.
