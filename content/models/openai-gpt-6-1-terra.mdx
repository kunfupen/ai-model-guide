## When to reach for GPT-6.1 Terra

GPT-6.1 Terra (October 26, 2026) is OpenAI's mid-tier GPT-6 model, built for
long-running, multi-step agentic work at **half the price of
[GPT-6.1 Sol](/models/openai-gpt-6-1-sol)**.

- Agent workloads where Sol's extra capability isn't worth 2× the bill.
- Computer use: OpenAI reports OSWorld 2.1 rising from 57.1 to 74.2 (max effort)
  over GPT-5.6 Terra.
- A big generational jump: Terminal-Bench 4.0 more than doubles over GPT-5.6
  Terra.

OpenAI itself still recommends **Sol or [GPT-6 Astra](/models/openai-gpt-6-astra)**
for the most complicated software-engineering tasks.

## Specs & cost

- **Model ID:** `gpt-6.1-terra` — confirmed in OpenAI's own OpenAPI spec.
- **Context:** ~1.05M tokens (up to 922K input), up to 128K output.
- **Pricing:** $1 / 1M input, $5 / 1M output, $0.05 / 1M cached input.
- **Long-context surcharge:** a request over **272K input tokens** is billed at
  2× input and 1.5× output **for the whole request** — check this before
  leaning on the full window.
- **Availability:** the OpenAI API, ChatGPT and Codex.

## Benchmark notes

**Terminal-Bench 4.0 (49.49)** is recorded from **Vals AI's independent run** —
the same source as GPT-6.1 Sol's 55.05 entry, so the two are directly
comparable. OpenAI's own figure is **50.3** at max effort; Artificial Analysis
reports 55.6. The spread is a reminder that this benchmark moves several points
with the harness and effort setting.

No SWE-bench Verified or GPQA Diamond score was published.
