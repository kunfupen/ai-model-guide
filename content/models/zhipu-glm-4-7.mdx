## When to reach for GLM-4.7

GLM-4.7 (December 22, 2025) is the direct successor to GLM-4.6 and the first
GLM tuned specifically for **multi-file agentic coding** rather than chat.

- MIT-licensed weights with no regional restrictions, on Hugging Face at
  `zai-org/GLM-4.7`.
- 200K context with up to 128K output — unusually generous output headroom for
  a model this size.

> Superseded by **[GLM-5.2](/models/zhipu-glm-5-2)** (open weights) and
> **[GLM-5.3](/models/zhipu-glm-5-3)** (current flagship, weights pending).

## Specs & licence

- **Architecture:** 355B total / ~32B active MoE.
- **Context:** 200K tokens, up to 128K max output.
- **Licence:** MIT.

## Benchmark notes

**No benchmark is recorded.** Z.ai reports **73.8% on SWE-bench Verified** (up
from 68.0% for GLM-4.6), and the release itself is confirmed by Z.ai's own
newswire announcements — but the only URL attesting that specific number is a
third-party technical write-up, not a first-party Z.ai page. Rather than anchor
a comparison row to that, the figure is stated here in prose only.
[Vals AI](https://www.vals.ai/models/zai_glm-4.7) carries independent
evaluations if you want third-party numbers.
