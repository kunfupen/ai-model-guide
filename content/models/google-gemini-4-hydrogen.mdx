## When to reach for Gemini 4 Hydrogen

Gemini 4 Hydrogen (November 12, 2026) is the fast, low-cost model of Google's
Gemini 4 generation. It replaces [Gemini 3.8 Flash](/models/google-gemini-3-8-flash)
as Google's everyday workhorse. Google calls it a daily driver for agentic work
and says it finishes tasks **2.5× faster than Gemini 3.8 Flash** at its default
reasoning setting.

- Repository coding and debugging, interactive web development, and multimodal
  workflows where speed and cost matter more than peak capability.
- Running alongside [Gemini 4 Carbon](/models/google-gemini-4-carbon) or
  [Gemini 4 Argon](/models/google-gemini-4-argon) as the cheap sub-agent tier.
- Tuning cost per task: four thinking levels, from low up to a new xhigh.

According to Google's model card, Hydrogen is adapted from Gemini 4 Carbon. It
is rolling out across the Gemini API, AI Studio, Gemini for business, the
Gemini app and Google Search.

## Specs & cost

- **Model ID:** `gemini-4-hydrogen`, as reported by launch coverage and API
  listings.
- **Context:** 1,048,576 tokens.
- **Pricing:** $1.50 / 1M input, $7.50 / 1M output, $0.075 / 1M cached input.
  That matches Gemini 3.8 Flash's standard rate. Batch and Flex run at half the
  standard rate.
- **Knowledge cutoff:** reported as March 2026.
