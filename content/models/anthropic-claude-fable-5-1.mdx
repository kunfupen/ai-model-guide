## When to reach for Claude Fable 5.1

Claude Fable 5.1 (September 1, 2026) is Anthropic's current top-end model,
positioned for **demanding reasoning and long-horizon agentic work** — the docs
recommend it specifically for when your evals on Claude Opus 5 at higher effort
still fall short.

- The hardest autonomous, multi-hour agentic runs.
- Heavily cached workloads: the cache-read price is the real story here.
- Scientific and long-horizon engineering work, where it more than doubles
  Fable 5's Terminal-Bench-Science score.

For most workloads **[Claude Opus 5](/models/anthropic-claude-opus-5)** remains
the recommended starting point at half the price, and it still holds this
catalog's top **SWE-bench Verified** score (97.0).

## The cache-read change

Base rates are unchanged from Fable 5 at **$10 / $50 per 1M**. What changed is
prompt caching: cache reads cost **$0.25 / 1M — 2.5% of the base input price**,
where every other Claude model charges 10%. That is a 75% cut on the line item
that dominates long agentic sessions, which is why reported savings land around
25% for typical token-billed work and up to ~45% for heavily agentic use.

## Specs & cost

- **Model ID:** `claude-fable-5-1` (same ID on Bedrock as `anthropic.claude-fable-5-1`, Vertex, and Microsoft Foundry).
- **Context:** 1M tokens, up to 128K output.
- **Thinking:** adaptive, always on. Default `effort` is `high`.
- **Knowledge cutoff:** June 2026.
- **Pricing:** $10 / 1M input, $50 / 1M output, $0.25 / 1M cache read.
- **Retirement:** not sooner than September 1, 2027.

### Behaviour changes worth knowing

- `tool_choice` accepts only `auto` and `none` — `any` and `tool` now return a
  400 error.
- Thinking blocks are readable only by the same model or newer.
- Generated media carries text watermarks and C2PA Content Credentials.
- Requires 30-day data retention; **not available under zero data retention.**

## Benchmark notes

**Anthropic's release notes publish no benchmark numbers for 5.1.** The two
figures recorded here — Terminal-Bench 4.0 (55.8, up from Fable 5's 42.0) and
Terminal-Bench-Science 0.1 (52.6, up from 24.7) — come from Anthropic's launch
comparison as relayed by third parties, not from a first-party page.

**No SWE-bench Verified score is recorded, deliberately.** Several write-ups
attribute **95.0** to Fable 5.1; that is
**[Fable 5's](/models/anthropic-claude-fable-5)** number, and Anthropic did not
headline a SWE-bench figure for 5.1. Note also that Terminal-Bench **4.0** is a
different, much harder benchmark than the Terminal-Bench **2.1** column used
elsewhere in this catalog — the two are not comparable.

## Mythos 5.1

As with Fable 5 and Mythos 5, **Claude Mythos 5.1** (`claude-mythos-5-1`) is the
same model under a different safety envelope, available to Project Glasswing
participants. It is not listed separately here.
