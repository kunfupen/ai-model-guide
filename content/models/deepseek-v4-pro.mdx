## When to reach for DeepSeek V4-Pro

DeepSeek V4-Pro (April 24, 2026) is the larger half of the V4 family — a
**1.6-trillion-parameter** mixture-of-experts model activating ~49B per token,
released under the **MIT licence** with weights on Hugging Face.

Its headline is price-to-performance: **80.6 on SWE-bench Verified**, the highest
open-weights score in this catalog and level with Gemini 3.1 Pro, at roughly a
thirtieth of frontier output pricing.

- Serious coding and reasoning where cost dominates — per output token it is
  ~29× cheaper than Claude Opus 4.8 and ~35× cheaper than GPT-5.5.
- Self-hosting or on-prem under a genuinely permissive licence.
- Very long context: 1M tokens with up to 384K output.

For lighter, higher-volume work the sibling **DeepSeek V4-Flash** is cheaper again.

## Specs & cost

- **Architecture:** 1.6T total parameters, MoE, ~49B active per token.
- **Context:** 1M tokens, up to 384K output.
- **Pricing:** $0.435 / 1M input and $0.87 / 1M output.
- **Licence:** MIT (open weights).

## Benchmark notes

The 80.6 SWE-bench Verified figure is reported for the model's **max-effort**
configuration (published as "V4-Pro-Max"), so it represents the top of its range
rather than default settings.
