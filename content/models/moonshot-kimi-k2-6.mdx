## When to reach for Kimi K2.6

Kimi K2.6 (April 20, 2026) is the release where Moonshot's agent story got
serious: a 1T-total / 32B-activated MoE that scales a **coding agent swarm to
roughly 300 sub-agents across ~4,000 coordinated steps**.

- Long-horizon coding tasks that need to be decomposed across many parallel
  workers rather than run as one long chain.
- Open-weight deployment under a Modified MIT licence.

> Superseded by **[Kimi K3](/models/moonshot-kimi-k3)** (July 2026), which is far
> larger and multimodal at 1M context. K2.6 remains the smaller, cheaper-to-host
> option in the same family.

## Specs & licence

- **Architecture:** 1T total / 32B activated MoE — 384 experts, 8 active plus 1
  shared per token.
- **Context:** 262,144 tokens.
- **Rollout:** reached subscribers around April 13, 2026 after a short closed
  beta; open weights landed on Hugging Face April 20, which is the date used
  here.
- **Licence:** Modified MIT.

## Benchmark notes

SWE-bench Verified (80.2) is recorded and is a launch-day vendor figure with no
independent replication at the time.

Moonshot also reported **SWE-bench Pro (58.6)** — a different, harder benchmark
that is not interchangeable with the Verified column, so it is not recorded. A
claimed HLE score of 54.0 "with tools" is omitted because the exact benchmark
variant could not be pinned down.

**Pricing is deliberately blank.** Sources conflict ($0.55/$2.65 vs $0.60/$2.50
per 1M) and the vendor pricing page could not be reached to settle it.
